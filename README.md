# BillSift

A local, privacy-first GST bill sorter for freelancers, small shops and their CAs.

Drop in photos or PDFs of bills. BillSift reads them with open-source OCR and a small
open-weight LLM running on your own laptop, then **checks the result with plain code**
(GSTIN checksum, tax maths, totals) before anything reaches your spreadsheet.

Work in progress, built step by step. Full docs coming in the final commit.
